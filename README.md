## About · 关于我

I am a humanities-background designer who moved from Japanese copywriting and translation into UI design through self-learning, and then into AI and Vibe Coding. I use AI to turn ideas into visual, interactive products that people can actually experience.

我是一名文科生设计师，从日语文案翻译出发，通过自学转向 UI 设计，再走进 AI 与 Vibe Coding。现在主要用 AI 把想法做成真正可以被看见、被体验的小产品，也持续分享非技术背景下的 AI 实践。
